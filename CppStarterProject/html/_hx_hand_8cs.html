◆ HandAnimationOptimizationMode

enum HandAnimationOptimizationMode

Different ways hand animation can be optimized.

Enumerator
DYNAMIC 

Optimizes for fingertip position when a finger is near the thumb, and joint angles otherwise.

JOINT_ANGLES 

Always optimizes for joint angles. Users may find that the avatar fingers don't touch when their real fingers do.

FINGERTIP_POSITIONS 

Always optimizes for fingertip positions. Users may find their knuckles scrunched when they should be straight.
