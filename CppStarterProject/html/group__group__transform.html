◆ calculateRotationalVelocity()

Vector3D HaptxApi::Quaternion::calculateRotationalVelocity ( const Quaternion & q0,
const Quaternion & qt,
float delta_time )
inlinestaticnodiscardnoexcept

Calculates the rotational velocity of an object that starts at q0 and ends at qt after delta_t seconds.

The result assumes that the angle traveled along the axis bridging the two input orientations is less than pi radians. Larger angular distances will result in aliased velocities that may even be in the opposite of the intended direction.

Parameters
q0The starting rotation (at time 0).
qtThe ending rotation (at time delta_t).
delta_timeThe amount of time in seconds that passed between the two rotations.
Returns
The rotational velocity. This is the rotational axis scaled by the magnitude of the rotation in radians per second.

Constructs a Quaternion from an Euler representation.

Rotation (and arguments) are applied in ZYX order.

Conventions are

  • yaw = psi = z_rad
  • pitch = theta = y_rad
  • roll = phi = x_rad
Parameters
