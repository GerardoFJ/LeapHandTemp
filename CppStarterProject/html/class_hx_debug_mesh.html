◆ DebugMeshType

enum HxDebugMesh.DebugMeshType : uint

The types of debug mesh that are supported.

Enumerator
CUBE_CENTERED 

A cube with its pivot at its center.

CUBE_GROUNDED 

A cube with its pivot at the center of its -Z face.

PLANE_CENTERED 

A plane with its pivot at its center.

PLANE_GROUNDED 

A plane with its pivot at the center of its -Z line.

SPHERE_CENTERED 

A sphere with its pivot at its center.

FIRST_VALUE 

Value of the first DebugMeshType.

LAST_VALUE 

Value of the last DebugMeshType.
