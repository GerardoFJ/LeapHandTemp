static constexpr Quaternion HaptxApi::JOINT1_ORIENT_CONVENTIONS [RD_LAST][F_LAST]
 Conventions for how we define motion in joints on each human hand. Each Quaternion is the rotation of the proximal joint on each finger in the palm's coordinate space where we consider the joint to be in its base/resting pose. These rotations match the base pose of our hand 3D models, and HaptX software team conventions for describing joint motion.
