◆ corrective_teleportation_distance_

float AHxHandActor::corrective_teleportation_distance_ {50.0f}

The max distance the hand can deviate from its tracked location before being teleported to it.

We automatically scale this when hand_scale_factor_ is greater than 1. Does not occur if enable_corrective_teleportation_ is false.

◆ dynamic_hand_anim_rel_dist_threshold_

float AHxHandActor::dynamic_hand_anim_rel_dist_threshold_ {0.5f}

The relative distance threshold to use if hand_anim_optimization_mode_ is set to EHandAnimationOptimizationMode::DYNAMIC.

A value of 0 effectively disables dynamic hand animation optimization and a value of 1 makes it engage as early as possible.

◆ enable_glove_slip_compensation_

bool AHxHandActor::enable_glove_slip_compensation_ {false}

Whether to automatically compensate for Glove slippage.

This subroutine kicks in when the user's fingers are out flat, and incrementally offsets the avatar's palm until its fingers are also out flat.

◆ enable_pinch_assist_

bool AHxHandActor::enable_pinch_assist_ {true}

Whether to automatically assist pinching.

This subroutine kicks in when the physical fingertips are close to the thumb tip and applies a flexion angle of the knuckle of those non-thumb fingers to reduce the gap.

◆ enable_thimble_compensation_

bool AHxHandActor::enable_thimble_compensation_ {false}

Whether to automatically compensate for thimble thickness.

This subroutine kicks in when the user's fingertips are close to the thumb tip and linearly reduces the gaps to zero.

◆ physics_authority_zone_radius_hysteresis_

float AHxHandActor::physics_authority_zone_radius_hysteresis_ {0.1f}
protected

As soon as the physics authority zone overlaps another physics authority zone the radius will increase by a multiplier equal to 1 plus this value. As soon as it is no longer overlapping any physics authority zones it will go back to its original size.

This prevents fluttering of state if physics authority zones are oscillating in and out of each other.
