Correct finger animation to be closer to pinching within a certain range.

Parameters
[in,out]anim_frameThe anim frame that will be updated with pinch assistance.
anim_profileThe user's bone lengths.
near_mThe distance between fingertips closer than which maximum pinch assistance is applied.
far_mThe distance between fingertips further than which no pinch assistance is applied.
effect_scaleThe amount of pinch assistance to apply. If set to 1, the maximum pinch assistance will attempt to overlap fingertip and thumb positions.
hand_scale_factorThe scale of the hand, which informs how we scale our pinch assistance bounds.
handThe handedness of our hand.
