Detailed Description

Utility struct encoding a real-valued coefficient (A) that multiplies an independent variable (x) raised to some integral exponent (B) to form a term in the form A*(x^B). The independent variable is not specified.

Note
Per its formal mathematical definition, a polynomial term cannot have a negative exponent; however, no such restriction is placed here.
